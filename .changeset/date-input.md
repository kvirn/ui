Add DateInput, a date of three text boxes: day, month and year (Plan 0013, Phase 3). Alpha.

- `@kvirn-ui/react`: `DateInput` (also `DateInput.Root`, `.Day`, `.Month` and `.Year`, each also a named export such as `DateInputRoot`) and `useDateInput(options)`. It goes inside a `Fieldset.Root` (use `group`) whose legend asks the question, with a `Fieldset.Hint` and a `Fieldset.ErrorMessage`. Each box is a Field with a visible label and a native `Input` (`inputMode="numeric"`, `spellCheck={false}`, no `maxLength`). The order follows the region through `Intl` (`sv-SE` year first, `sv-FI`, `fi`, `nb` and `en-GB` day first) and a month-first result (`en`, `en-US`) becomes day first. A Root without children renders the boxes in that order, and `useDateInput().order` exposes it so the hint's example can follow it. Write the parts yourself for an order of your own. Three Tab stops, no auto-advance, the arrow keys never step a value, and the value is `{ year, month, day }` as strings that nothing parses. `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange(value, { reason: 'input', part, event })` on the Root, `name` as a prefix (`birth-day`, `birth-month`, `birth-year`), `autoComplete="bday"` for the three `bday-*` tokens, `required`, `disabled` and `readOnly` (the first two default to the Fieldset's), and `invalid` per box, or `invalidParts` on the Root. The Fieldset's `invalid` marks none of the boxes. Dev warnings for a Root outside a group, a box outside a Root, and a Root in a Fieldset that is neither `group` nor `required`. KvirnUI holds no form state, supplies no hint text and never validates the date.
- `@kvirn-ui/i18n`: the `dateInput` namespace in all six locales: `day`, `month` and `year`, the labels of the boxes. `fi`, `nb` and `nn` are drafts for a translator, and Northern Sámi starts as English placeholders (`TODO(native-review)`).
- `@kvirn-ui/theme`: `kv-date-input`, a row of boxes that wraps (16px between them, 12px in `kv-compact` from 64rem), and `kv-date-input-day`, `-month` and `-year` on each box's field, which size the box by what it holds (two characters, two, four) whatever order the boxes are rendered in. The boxes are plain `kv-input` with tabular figures, and their labels are weight 400. No new tokens.
- `@kvirn-ui/theme`: `kv-heading` now hyphenates and breaks long words (`hyphens: auto`, `hyphenate-limit-chars`, `overflow-wrap: break-word`, as prose and `kv-field` do), so a long compound in a heading outside prose never scrolls a 320px page sideways (1.4.10). Approved by the maintainer on 2026-10-04 (DESIGN.md lists headings in the text-expansion rule).
