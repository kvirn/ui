Tabs (Plan 0048): a list of tabs and a panel for each, one tab selected and its panel shown (APG Tabs).

- React: `Tabs.Root`, `Tabs.List`, `Tabs.Tab` and `Tabs.Panel`, a namespace of its own parts like `Navigation`, with the flat exports `TabsRoot`, `TabsList`, `TabsTab` and `TabsPanel`. The hook `useTabs({ value, defaultValue, onValueChange, activationMode, orientation })` returns `rootProps`, `listProps`, `getTabProps(value, { disabled, onClick })` and `getPanelProps(value)` for your own elements. One of `value` (controlled) and `defaultValue` is required by the types. `onValueChange(value, { reason, event })` reports `press`, `arrow-key` or `home-end-key`. Types: `UseTabsOptions`, `UseTabsResult`, `UseTabsTabOptions`, `TabsRootProps`, `TabsListProps`, `TabsTabProps`, `TabsPanelProps`, `TabsState`, `TabsTabState`, `TabsPanelState`, `TabsActivationMode`, `TabsChangeReason`, `TabsChangeDetails` and the part prop types.
- The tab list is one Tab stop (a roving `tabindex`) at the selected tab, and the arrow keys, Home and End move between the tabs, wrapping, flipped in right-to-left text (Down and Up when `orientation="vertical"`). `activationMode="automatic"` (the default) selects the tab that gets focus, and `"manual"` leaves that to Enter and Space. A disabled tab stays focusable with `aria-disabled="true"` and is never selected. Every panel is rendered, `hidden` while it isn't selected, so every `aria-controls` resolves. A panel has `tabindex="0"` by default (an APG deviation, in the contract): pass `tabIndex={-1}` to a panel that starts with a focusable element. Ids come from `useId()` and the value, so they are right on the first server render.
- Development warnings: `tabs-<part>-outside-root`, `tabs-tab-not-a-button:<element>`, `tabs-value-without-tab:<value>` and `tabs-unpaired:<value>`.
- Theme: `kv-tabs`, `kv-tabs-list`, `kv-tabs-tab` and `kv-tabs-panel` (section 8a). A quiet tab in the text colour, the selected one in weight 600 with a straight 4px `primary` bar at its block-end edge (inline-end edge when vertical), a hairline along the list, a focus ring on a tab and on the panel, and a vertical layout from `data-orientation="vertical"` that stacks below 40rem. The list wraps and never scrolls. `CanvasText` bar, `ButtonText` text and `Highlight` ring in forced colours. No new token.
