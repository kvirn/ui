Add Notification, the status message, and one vocabulary for Surface, Section, Card and Notification (Plan 0020).

- `@kvirn-ui/react`: `Notification` with `Root`, `Info`, `Success`, `Warning`, `Danger`, `Title`, `Body` and `Actions` (also the named exports `NotificationRoot`, `NotificationInfo`, `NotificationSuccess`, `NotificationWarning`, `NotificationDanger`, `NotificationTitle`, `NotificationBody` and `NotificationActions`), and `useNotification({ variant?, announce?, messages? })`. Status is a class, not a prop: the plain `Notification.Root` renders `kv-notification` only, and each ready-made root renders its status class (`kv-notification--info|success|warning|danger`), its decorative icon (`info`, `success`, `warning`, `error`) and the status word at the start of the Title, all from one table, so the colour, the icon and the word can't disagree. The Title is required, an `h2` by default (`render` sets the level or a `<p>`). A root adds no `role`, `aria-live`, `aria-atomic` or `tabindex`. `announce="polite|assertive"` on any root makes one Announcer call on mount with the Title and Body text, and nothing is announced without it. Development warnings (silent in production) for a missing Title, a part outside a root, a live role or `aria-live` on a root, `announce="assertive"` on Info or Success, a status class of another status on a ready-made root, and one of our status classes on the plain Root. Types: `NotificationVariant`, `UseNotificationOptions`, `UseNotificationResult`, `NotificationRootProps`, `NotificationStatusRootProps`, `NotificationTitleProps`, `NotificationBodyProps`, `NotificationActionsProps`, `NotificationElementProps`, `NotificationState` and the `Notification*PartProps` types.
- `@kvirn-ui/i18n`: the namespace `notification` with four keys, `infoPrefix`, `successPrefix`, `warningPrefix` and `dangerPrefix`, in all six locales. The fi, nb and nn words are drafts for a translator to confirm. The `se` (Northern Sámi) words are machine-drafted and need a native speaker to verify them.
- `@kvirn-ui/theme`: notification styles. The look is only CSS on the status class, through two component tokens, `--kv-notification-background` and `--kv-notification-accent`: a `-subtle` background, a 4px inline-start bar, the `sm` radius, no shadow and no motion. A bare `kv-notification` is a neutral surface, so your own status class only sets the two tokens. New site-wide properties `--kv-notification-padding-block`, `--kv-notification-padding-inline`, `--kv-notification-gap`, `--kv-notification-title-size` and `--kv-notification-title-line-height`. The status word is visually hidden, the box keeps its border in forced colours, and a notification is a prose boundary like a card. `kv-notification` and its parts are public class names. No new colour and no new contrast pair: `checkThemeCss()` now also holds a tinted button edge to 3:1 on the four notification backgrounds (`buttonEdgePairsPerTheme` is now 56).
- Docs: "Section, Card, Notification or a surface token: when to use which", and the vocabulary in DESIGN.md. "Panel", "banner" and "callout" are no longer used for a status.
