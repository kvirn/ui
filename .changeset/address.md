Address (Plan 0095).

- `Address`: contact details of the page or an article, as `<address class="kv-address">`. No role, ARIA, text or strings, and no `as`. Attributes and the ref reach the element, and a `className` joins `kv-address`. Type: `AddressProps`.
- `@kvirn-ui/theme`: `kv-address` undoes the browser's italic and keeps the body text size and line height.
