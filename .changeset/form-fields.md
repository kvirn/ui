Form fields, phases 1 and 1b (Plan 0013). KvirnUI holds no form state: the components render what they're given and report changes up, so TanStack Form, React Hook Form or a plain `<form>` own the values and the validity.

- `react`: `Field` (`Root`, `Label`, `Description`, `ErrorMessage`), `Fieldset` (`Root`, `Legend`, `Description`, `ErrorMessage`) and `Input`, each part also as a named export, with the hooks `useField`, `useFieldset` and `useInput`. A label names the control, the hint and the error are in its `aria-describedby`, `invalid` sets `aria-invalid`, and `required` sets `aria-required`. A field that isn't required ends its label with "(optional)". The error starts with a visually hidden "Error:" prefix and an icon. Numbers are `Input` with `inputMode`, never `type="number"`.
- `react`: field order and several descriptions. The default order is label, hint, control, a second hint under the control, then the error. A Field or Fieldset can have several `Description`s, each with its own id, and `aria-describedby` lists them in DOM order, then the error. `useField` and `useFieldset` take `descriptions` (names) and return `getDescriptionProps(name)`, so server-rendered markup is complete, and `hasDescription` and `descriptionProps` still work. A dev warning fires when a Field or Fieldset renders two `ErrorMessage`s.
- `react`: `InputGroup` (`Root`, `Addon`), also as `InputGroupRoot` and `InputGroupAddon`, and `useInputGroup`: a unit, an icon or a Button inside the input's box. The Root takes `data-invalid` and `data-disabled` from the Field and sets `data-focus-visible` while the Input has keyboard focus. An Addon is `aria-hidden`, and clicking it focuses the Input. A dev warning fires when an Addon contains focusable content.
- `theme`: styles for the form fields (`kv-field`, `kv-field-label`, `kv-field-description`, `kv-field-error-message`, `kv-fieldset`, `kv-fieldset-legend` and `kv-input`), the width classes `kv-input--width-2|4|6|10|20`, `kv-input--numeric`, the heading modifiers `kv-field-label--heading` and `kv-fieldset-legend--heading`, an invalid, disabled and read-only look, forced colours and compact density. New tokens: `--kv-field-gap`, `--kv-input-padding-inline` and `--kv-control-border-width-invalid`. An input group (`kv-input-group`, `kv-input-group-addon`) draws the input's box with the focus ring around the whole group, a 2px `danger` edge when invalid with no content shift, and a Button inside it as a flat, full-height segment with a divider. `theme:check` also holds `danger` to 3:1 as an edge on every background, and `primary` on the status panels. Prose leaves fields and fieldsets alone.
- `i18n`: `field.optional` and `field.errorPrefix` in all six languages. The se text is English.
