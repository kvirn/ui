Add Card (Plan 0007, ADR-0020), and keep a `primary` edge on a hovered primary button (ADR-0021).

- `@kvirn-ui/react`: `Card.Root`, `Card.Header`, `Card.Body` and `Card.Footer` (also `CardRoot`, `CardHeader`, `CardBody` and `CardFooter`), and `useCard()` with `rootProps`, `headerProps`, `bodyProps` and `footerProps`. A plain container: each part renders one `<div>` with its class, `kv-card`, `kv-card-header`, `kv-card-body` or `kv-card-footer`, and no role, ARIA, text or behaviour. A `className` prop or a `render` element's own class joins the part's class, never replaces it. Header and Footer are never `<header>` or `<footer>`. `render` changes the element (`<article>`, `<section aria-labelledby>`, `<li>`), refs are forwarded, and `className` and `style` merge. `useCard()`'s props hold only the part's `className`. Types: `CardRootProps`, `CardHeaderProps`, `CardBodyProps`, `CardFooterProps`, `CardElementProps` (what a `render` function spreads), `CardState`, `CardPartProps` and `UseCardResult`.
- `@kvirn-ui/theme`: card styles. Elevation level 2: always `surface-raised` (a region of the page is a Section, ADR-0044), with a 1px `border-subtle` edge, the `lg` radius and no shadow, and modifier classes: `kv-card--radius-md` and `kv-card--radius-none` (the default is `lg`), `kv-card--padding-none`, `-sm` and `-lg` on the Root (the default is `md`), `kv-card-header--padding-*`, `kv-card-body--padding-*` and `kv-card-footer--padding-*` (`none`, `sm`, `md`, `lg`) on a part, and `kv-card--dividers`. New tokens `--kv-card-padding-sm`, `--kv-card-padding-md` and `--kv-card-padding-lg`: `md` is 24px, and 16px below 40rem and in compact density. Full-bleed media get the card's inner corners, and the card never hides overflow, so focus rings aren't clipped.
- `@kvirn-ui/theme`: prose stops at a card. A card in prose gets prose's block margins and nothing inside it is prose-styled, unless `kv-prose` is on the card or inside it.
- `@kvirn-ui/theme`: a hovered or pressed primary button keeps a 1px `primary` border instead of a transparent one, so its edge stays at 3:1 on `surface-raised` in dark. `contrastRequirements` now requires `danger-hover` at 3:1 on `canvas`, `surface` and `surface-raised`.
