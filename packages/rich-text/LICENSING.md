# Licensing

> **Draft: not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Do not rely on this text until it has been.

**KvirnUI is source-available. Anyone can read, copy, change and try it. Using it in production is free for individuals' personal use. Any other production use needs a commercial licence.**

## Free for everyone

Reading, copying, changing, testing and evaluating the code is free for everyone, including companies and public bodies. So is any use that is not production use: development, testing, staging and evaluation environments need no licence. You do not need to ask. Only running it in production for an organisation, or for payment, needs a commercial licence.

## Personal use (free in production)

You, as an individual, may use KvirnUI in production for yourself: personal projects, hobby sites and learning. It must not be for payment, and not for or on behalf of an employer, a client, a company, a non-profit, a public body, a school or any other organisation.

## Any other production use (licence required)

Production use by or for an organisation of any kind (companies, non-profits, government and other public bodies, schools), and any work done for payment, whether as an employee, a freelancer or an agency, needs a **commercial licence**. The licence also covers support terms.
