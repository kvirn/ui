# Accessibility contract: Calendar

- **APG pattern:** [Date Picker Dialog](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/examples/datepicker-dialog/), its grid and keys. A standalone Calendar is the grid with its month buttons; the modal dialog belongs to DatePicker.
- **Deviations:** none from the APG keys. Range mode (approved by the maintainer 2026-10-06) has no APG pattern and adds no key: a range is two ordinary selections. Decisions, also approved on 2026-10-06: the month is announced through the Announcer on a button press only, not by a live heading; days of other months are empty cells; the year buttons are optional parts; below 40rem wide the day cells are smaller than the comfortable 44 pixels so that seven columns fit a 320 pixel screen (2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum) (AA) is met there; 2.5.5 Target Size (Enhanced) (AAA) is not).
- **Native elements used:** `<table role="grid">` with `<th scope>` headers, `<td role="gridcell">` days, `<button>` month and year buttons, `<h3>`, `<p>`.
- **Status:** alpha candidate. Reviewer findings fixed, accessibility-reviewer re-review pending. Manual AT is `pending`.
- **Tests:** `calendar.test.tsx` next to this file. `calendar.stories.tsx` in `apps/storybook/src/components/calendar/`. The date arithmetic is tested in `@kvirn-ui/core` (`calendar-keys.test.ts`).

A month as a grid of days for choosing one date near today (a booking, a start date), or with `mode="range"` a start and an end, one or two months side by side. It is a companion to typed entry (DateInput), never the only way to give a date. Calendar holds the visible month, the focused day and the chosen day in a core store; the consumer owns the chosen value (`value` and `onValueChange`, or `defaultValue`). In range mode the value is `{ start, end }` (`''` for none; a start alone is valid) and `onValueChange(range, change)` runs after every press that changed it.

Rules, each proved by an automated test:

- **The grid is named and described.** By the Heading, and by the RangeHint when there is a range. A missing Heading or RangeHint warns once (`calendar-heading-missing`, `calendar-range-hint-missing`).
- **Status is never colour alone.** Selected is a fill, bold text and `aria-selected`; today is an edge, bold text and the name; unavailable is a strike-through, `aria-disabled` and the name.
- **Min and max.** A key's target is clamped to the range. Days outside it are `aria-disabled` and are not selectable. A `minimum` after the `maximum` throws a `RangeError`.
- **Range mode.** A press is the start, then the end (`started`, `completed`); a day that can't be the end (before the start, too short, too long, past an unavailable day) becomes the new start and is announced, so there is never a silent dead end. `selects="start"` or `"end"` is one end of a from/to pair sharing one range; an impossible end of `selects="end"` is `aria-disabled` and `data-unavailable`, with its reason in the name. The span is counted in inclusive days (`minimumDays`, `maximumDays`); an unavailable day blocks the range unless `allowUnavailableInRange`. The preview (hover, focus) is drawn only: the length is in each candidate's name, not announced. `aria-selected` on every day in the range is the maintainer's choice; how screen readers voice it is a question for the manual AT run.
- **Two months** show side by side from 64rem (`visibleMonths={2}` is a maximum, one on the server, then the media query), one Tab stop and one focused day across both grids; one below 64rem so it never breaks 320px or 400% zoom.
- **Limits.** `minimumDays` or `maximumDays` below 1, or a minimum above the maximum, is ignored with a development warning (`calendar-range-days-invalid`).
- **Week numbers** are ISO 8601 and need a Monday start; another start hides them and warns once (`calendar-week-numbers-need-monday`).
- **Month and weekday names** come from `Intl` with the Gregorian calendar and Latin digits.

## Keyboard

- **Selection follows focus:** no (Enter or Space selects)
- **Arrows wrap:** no (they cross into the next or previous month)
- **Shortcuts:** none

The month and year buttons are Tab stops; the grid is one Tab stop, on the focused day (the chosen day, else the typed day, else today, kept inside the range). A key with Control, Alt or Meta is never taken.

## Focus management

- Initial focus: not moved. The Tab stop is the chosen day, else `defaultFocusedDate`, else today, clamped to the range.
- After a key move: focus follows to the new day's cell, in the new month when it crosses. After a month or year button: focus stays on the button.
- Trap: no (a standalone Calendar). DatePicker's modal Dialog traps.
- Restore to: not applicable. DatePicker returns focus to its trigger.
- Never obscured by: the focus ring is drawn outside the cell, in the gap between cells, so it never covers a neighbouring day (2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) (AA), 2.4.13 Focus Appearance (AAA)).

## Consumer responsibilities

- Offer typed entry beside the Calendar (a DateInput): the Calendar is slower for keyboard and screen reader users. Don't use it for a date of birth, and prefer radios when many days are unavailable.
- Say why a day is unavailable in `getDateDescription`, and keep the visible range text (RangeHint) when there is a `minimum` or `maximum`.
- Render the Heading; it names the grid.
- Set `weekStart` on the KvirnProvider (or the Calendar) explicitly for server rendering: the locale-derived default reads `Intl`, which can differ between server and browser.
- Wrap the app in a KvirnProvider: it holds the Announcer.

## Visual / modes

- Focus indicator: a ring at least 2 pixels thick, with at least 3:1 contrast against its surroundings (2.4.13 Focus Appearance, AAA), drawn in the gap between cells. If you restyle the Calendar, keep the ring visible and keep the gap, so it never covers a neighbouring day.
- Target size: day cells are the size of other controls on wide screens, smaller in the compact density, and smaller again below 40rem wide so that seven columns fit a 320 pixel screen (a recorded exception to the comfortable 44 pixels). Every size is above the 24 by 24 pixel minimum of 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum) (AA). Month buttons are the size of other controls.
- Forced colours (a user's high-contrast mode) uses the system's text and highlight colours: today has a text-coloured edge, the chosen day is filled with the highlight colour, an unavailable day is greyed out with a strike-through, and hover is a highlight-coloured edge. The edges are borders, because forced colours drops a shadow.
- Range, forced colours: the start and end look like a chosen day. The days between have highlight-coloured edges and no fill, and the preview is dashed. Hover inside the band adds a highlight-coloured border, and an unavailable day in an allowed range is greyed out with a strike-through. Never colour alone: the start and end have a fill, bold text, a shape and a name, and the band has edges and `aria-selected`.
- Reduced motion: no motion.
- Right to left: the columns and the chevrons mirror, and the arrow keys follow the reading direction.

## WCAG SCs covered

- 1.3.1 Info and Relationships (A), 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (A): the grid, headers and gridcells, names and states.
- 1.4.1 Use of Color (A), 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA): the cues for selected, today and unavailable.
- 1.4.10 Reflow (AA): a story at 320 pixels wide checks that nothing scrolls sideways, with and without week numbers, and in range mode.
- 1.4.12 Text Spacing (AA): no automated check.
- 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus (AA): the name of a month or year button also shows as a tooltip, which follows the Tooltip's rules (dismissable, hoverable, persistent).
- 2.1.1 Keyboard (A), 2.1.4 Character Key Shortcuts (A, there are none), 2.4.3 Focus Order (A), 2.4.7 Focus Visible (AA), 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) (AA).
- 2.5.3 Label in Name (A), 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum) (AA).
- 3.1.2 Language of Parts (AA): `lang` on a month written in a fallback language.
- 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions (A): the range hint, the span limits and the next step.
- 4.1.3 Status Messages (AA): the month, the chosen day, the start of a range and a finished range.

## Known issues

- **A controlled `value` that changes while mounted** moves the Tab stop and the visible month to it. A change of `minimum` or `maximum` that moves the focused day to another month keeps focus in the grid.
- **"Today" is read once, on mount.** A Calendar open across midnight keeps yesterday as today.
- **Without a provider `timeZone`, the first paint uses the UTC date.** Server and hydrated HTML agree; after hydration the grid switches "today" (`aria-current="date"`) to the browser's date, and the Tab stop moves with it while the grid is untouched (nothing chosen, no `defaultFocusedDate`) and no cell holds DOM focus; DOM focus never moves. A Calendar mounted after hydration starts on the browser's date and does not switch. Pass `timeZone` on `KvirnProvider` for the same day on the server and the client. A passed `today` or a provider `timeZone` is never changed.
- **Empty cells are `aria-hidden`.** A screen reader's table commands may count fewer cells in the first and last week than the seven columns.
