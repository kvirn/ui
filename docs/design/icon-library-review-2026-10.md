# Icon library review, 2026-10-17

`ux-designer` review of the 256 icons in `@kvirn-ui/icons` before release (Plan 0124, Task 8), with the open design questions the maintainer delegated on 2026-10-17. Scope: the most obvious problems only. No redraw of the set; similar icons and variants are fine; the 45°/90°, named 1:2 and 2:1 slopes and `new`'s 28° points are accepted.

**What was looked at:** every file in `packages/icons/svg/` rendered in Chromium from `catalog.json`, per category at 16, 20, 24, 32 and 48px (1× and 2×), the whole set at 24px light and dark, 20px dark, 20px and 16px greyscale, and 16/20/24/96px zooms of the candidates below. The spinner stories were screenshotted from a running Storybook with and without reduced motion. Recognition by residents, forced colours and AT were **not** checked: they are `pending`.

## Overall

The set reads as one family: one stroke, radius 2 containers, the same live area, and the variants (add, remove, off) are consistent. The status shapes (square, circle, triangle, octagon) hold apart in greyscale at 20px. Keep this: the fixes below are local.

## 2. Decisions

| #   | Question                                   | Decided                                                                                                                                                       | Because                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2   | Add/remove host rule                       | **Share the host.** A `-remove` uses its `-add` sibling's host drawing and position exactly; only the badge differs. Table remove hosts move 1 up (fix list) | A pair then differs only in + and −, which is what the user compares, and swapping one for the other in place doesn't jump. One rule the checker can test (identical host path). Already true for pin, mail, image, person, calendar, document, folder. 1 unit off-centre is 0.83px at 20px: not visible |
| 3   | `building-add`, `building-remove`          | **Keep cut.** No "Building, badged" kit part                                                                                                                  | No component or e-service in the catalogue needs them (the catalogue's own cut test). A kit part for two icons widens the rules. "Add building" in a staff tool is a text button. Reopen on a named need                                                                                                  |
| 4   | Near-misses `ambulance`, `signature`, `warning`, `quick-exit`, `bike` (1.5–1.86 clear) | **Accept**, as a named tolerance: a clear gap of 1.5 to 2 is allowed where it is listed here                                        | A meaningful icon is 20px or larger, where 1.5 units is 1.25px: the gap stays open, and it did at 20 and 24px on the sheets. At 16px these are decorative. Any gap measured under 1.5 is a redraw, not covered by this                                                                                     |

### 8. Recognition tests before release (pending)

1. `quick-exit`: safety-critical (a page that must close fast); must not read as "sign out" or "running". Recruit with care, through partner organisations.
2. `help`: the lifebuoy without a "?" is a deliberate departure; it must read as help or support, not swimming.
3. `text-size`, `contrast`, `dark-mode` as one task (the display settings).
4. `bold` and `italic` as bars, in the editor toolbar (the set's riskiest pair).
5. `wheelchair` (the chair as an object, not the ISA): recognition and acceptability with wheelchair users.

Separately, a sensitivity review with affected groups (not a recognition test): `gender-*`, `benefit` (can read as alms), `accessibility` (close to the UN logo). Participants for all: residents with low digital confidence, second-language speakers, screen-magnifier users, older adults. Nothing here has happened.

### 9. Spinner story (`apps/storybook/src/components/icons/loading-spinner.stories.tsx`)

- **MAJOR** `InContext`: a Progress with its own spinner beside a busy Button shows two moving indicators for one wait. The spec says never (§5): the Progress beside a busy Button carries the words without a spinner. Fix: drop the spinner from that Progress, or split the story.
- **MINOR** `Default`, `InContext`, `Rtl`: the label is a `<span>` with an inline font size, not the documented lockup (`kv-progress-label`, a `<p>`), so the story doesn't show the real first-line alignment. Fix: use the lockup (or `Progress`) and add a long, wrapping label (the longest `fi` string, a marked exception).
- **MINOR** The colour control offers `danger` and `success`. A spinner in a status colour suggests a status it doesn't have (spec L6: `primary`, `currentColor`, the alert accent). Fix: `primary` and `current` only.
- **Works well:** `aria-hidden`, no `progressbar` role, words always beside it, it stays at the inline start in RTL, and the reduced-motion rest shape is the ¾ arc (never the full circle of `success`), as the spec says.
- **Not judged:** one capture under reduced motion, 0.7s after load, showed a short dash in place of the ¾ arc; captures after 2s, and the computed values (head 270°, tail 0°, no animation), show the ¾ arc. Not reproduced: a person should watch the first paint under reduced motion.
