# Icon catalogue

Every icon of `@kvirn-ui/icons`: **257 icons, all drawn, none planned**. Priority (1 to 3) ordered the drawing until an icon was drawn, and is removed from a drawn icon, so the Priority column is `–` throughout.

## How the catalogue is used

- **`packages/icons/catalog.json` is the source of truth.** The docs site's Icons page reads it: search over name, aliases and tags, and a filter by category. This file is a readable view of it, written by hand from the JSON now and generated by a script later. When the two disagree, the JSON wins: fix this file.
- **Drawing facts live elsewhere.** The grid, keylines and markup rules are `docs/design/icon.md` §6.1; the parts kit, construction notes, QA facts and the inventory of drawn icons (concept, keyline, ink class) are `docs/design/icon-library.md`. A `drawn` icon has a file in `packages/icons/svg/`; a `planned` one is a name, a metaphor and a decision, not a drawing.
- **Fields.** `name` (kebab-case, by meaning); `category` (exactly one; cross-cutting search uses `tags`); `aliases` (second registry names for the same drawing, never a second drawing); `tags` (3 to 8 plain English search words); `meaning` (what it means, then what it depicts); `status` (`drawn` or `planned`); `priority` (planned only, applied until the icon is drawn: 1 = needed by an existing component, pattern or e-service now, 2 = a common public-sector need, 3 = nice to have); `mirrorInRtl`; `label` (the i18n key `icons.<camelName>` for an icon that may stand alone with meaning, `null` when the icon is always part of a control or link that carries the name).
- **Rules every entry passes.** No letters, numerals, flags, brands or emblems; names by meaning, `back`/`forward` for reading direction and compass names on the map; no two entries share a name or an alias, and no alias equals another icon's name; no badged icon mirrors. Recognition testing with residents is `pending` for every icon.
- **Labels** in the tables are proposals: the key and its English text. The engineer adds the five locales when an icon is drawn. A meaningful or status icon is used at 20px or larger.

## RTL decisions

The decisions are in `icon-library.md` §6 and `mirror-in-rtl.json`. An icon mirrors when it shows a horizontal direction in reading order:

- `first`, `last`, `arrow-back`, `arrow-forward`, `chevron-back`, `chevron-forward`: they go to the start and the end of a sequence in reading order.
- `send`: the paper plane flies to the inline end. `sign-in`, `sign-out`: the arrow enters and leaves towards the inline end. `undo`, `redo`, `external`, `quick-exit`, `delegation`, `change-of-address`: a direction or a hand-over in reading order.
- Text editing: `align-start`, `align-end`, `bullet-list`, `numbered-list`, `indent`, `outdent`, `quote`.
- `translate` mirrors: its arrow is one-way, from the back panel to the front one, and reads in order.

Directional-looking icons that don't mirror: `refresh`, `repeat` and `history` (rotation is not language, and `history` holds a clock); `play`, `skip-next` and `skip-previous` (follow the media, not the script); `expand-all`, `collapse-all` (vertical); `chart-line`, `chart-bar` (charts never mirror); `car`, `bus`, `train`, `tram`, `ferry`, `bike`, `walk`, `edit`, `signature`, `video-call`, `read-aloud` (objects facing a side). `refund` doesn't mirror: its U-turn arrow follows the story (the money comes back to you), not the script. `detour` doesn't mirror: it is a route drawing, like `directions`. No badged icon (`person-add`, `calendar-add`, `calendar-check`, `document-check`, `notification-off`, `pin-remove`) mirrors.

## Pairs to check when drawing

## Cuts and merges

**Cut: needs a letter, numeral, flag, brand or emblem.**

`tax`, `passport`, `easy-read`, `hearing-loop`, `audio-description`, `shelter`, `toilet`, `entrance`, `parking` and `charging` stay cut (unchanged, below).

- `tax`: without a currency or per-cent sign it is a document with words. Use `document` or `invoice`.
- `parking`: the sign is a "P". Use the words, with `car` if an icon is wanted.
- `easy-read`: the national easy-to-read marks are registered marks of their owners; a generic drawing would compete with them. The consumer supplies the mark.
- `hearing-loop`: the standard sign carries a "T".
- `audio-description`: the standard mark is the letters "AD".
- `pdf`, `doc`, file-type icons: letters. `document` with the type in words.
- `police` as insignia or a badge: national emblems. Reopened on 2026-10-16 and drawn as a patrol car (below).

- `report` as a flag: no flags. A fault report is `repair`, a written report is `form`.
- `euro`, `currency`, `savings` (piggy bank): currency symbols and an animal mascot.

**Cut: abstract, two ideas, or no component or e-service needs it.**

- `wellbeing`, `mental-health`: no concrete object; a heart or a leaf needs decoding.

## Decisions (maintainer, 2026-10-16)

- **Status shapes.** The four containers stay the only status shapes: `info` (rounded square), `success` (circle), `warning` (triangle), `error` (octagon). `pending`, `in-progress`, `blocked`, `new`, `attention` and `help` are glyph-only, with no container, and each always comes with words (1.4.1). The lifebuoy of `help` is the object itself, not a container.
- **`contrast`** is a circle with its right half drawn as diagonal stripes: outline only, no fill and no CSS fill. When it is drawn, `DisplaySettings.Compact` moves from its inline half-filled circle to it (an engineer change).
- **Shapes:** `dark-mode` is a half-moon disc (a circle with an inner arc, not a crescent), `help` a lifebuoy ring and `text-size` stepped bars. `sign-language` was tried and cut (above).
- **Aliases:** `navigation` belongs to `menu` only. `download` adds `save` and `export`, `upload` adds `import`, `calendar` adds `event`, `add` adds `zoom-in`, `remove` adds `zoom-out`.
- **`marker`** stays out of the catalogue (above).
- **Later, for an engineer:** Table's inline sort indicator (chevrons, `packages/react/src/table/table.tsx`) and the library's `sort` (two arrows) are aligned in a later change.
- **No icon budget (2026-10-16).** The 170 indicative budget is dropped and every catalogued icon is drawn: 257 (with `spinner`). The `leisure-culture` category is added. `school` moved out of `education`'s aliases: `school` is a building, `university` the cap, `education` an open book.
- **Follow-up list (2026-10-16).** 23 icons asked for by the maintainer were added. Brand logos, `like`, `heart` and `star` stay out. `skip-next` and `skip-previous` do not mirror, like `play`; `translate` mirrors.
- **Add, remove and off variants (2026-10-16).** 17 were requested, 14 drawn and 3 cut (`building-add`, `building-remove`, `phone-off`). Badged and slashed variants never mirror. `document-add` and `document-remove` have no text line (a minus beside a text line reads as two lines at 16px). `image-add` and `image-remove` have no sun (no host position fits the ring 2 clear of the plus).
