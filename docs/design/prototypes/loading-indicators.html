Loading indicators: spinner and gradient bar

Prototype for docs/design/loading-indicators.md. Text stays first: every indicator here is decorative (aria-hidden), and the words beside it carry the meaning. Loops are endless; one page-level "Stop animations" toggle (2.2.2) stops every indicator on the page and leaves its rest shape: a three-quarter arc, or the static hatch. Only A1 arc and B1 sweep ship; the others are kept for comparison, marked "Not shipping".

Spinner: grow and shrink variants (A1 look, motion only)

Same ring, stroke, size, colour and tokens as A1; only the motion differs. Each shows lg and md. Close and reopen this section to restart them all together. Stop and reduced motion show the same three-quarter arc for every variant.

A Baseline (today's §6.2)

Breathes 0.75 → 0.2 → 0.75 in place on the standard easing while the box turns every 0.8 s.

Recommended

B Crawl

Head on the standard easing, tail on an ease-in-out: the head leads, the tail catches up, and the arc crawls forward.

C Inchworm

The head stretches, then the tail follows, one at a time, ease-in-out cubic.

D Deep breath

Breathes 0.85 ↔ 0.1, ease-in-out cubic: more drama, still in place.

E Gentle breath

Breathes 0.7 ↔ 0.25, sine in-out, 2 s: the calmest of the breathing ones.

F Drift

Turns every 1.4 s against a 1.6 s breath, so the short arc lands somewhere new each time.

G Overshoot

Each half overshoots its target a little, then settles: springy.

H Stepped turn

The box turns in 12 steps with a smooth breath: a clock-tick feel.

I Fixed arc

No grow: a 0.3-turn arc turning every 0.8 s. The cheapest (compositor only).

J Calm crawl

B, slower: a 2.2 s cycle and a 3.2 s turn.
