Button depth: five gentle variations

An exploration for the project owner, not a decision. DESIGN.md still says the look is flat. Spec, values, measurements and open questions: docs/design/button-depth.md.

  • Every variation keeps the label on the measured token colour, or on a darker stop of it. Nothing is ever lighter behind a white label: even 4% white over primary drops it to 4.37:1.
  • Edges keep 3:1 (1.4.11), the focus ring is 2px with a 2px offset (4px in E, past its ledge), hover darkens filled buttons, and every button is at least 44px high.
  • Disabled stays flat and dashed in every variation, so losing the depth is one more cue.
  • Hover, pressed and focus are forced in the grid. The buttons in each card are live: hover, press and Tab to them.
  • Forced colours: emulate them in DevTools (Rendering, "Emulate CSS media feature forced-colors"). Reduced motion: the same panel, prefers-reduced-motion.

The current theme, for comparison. No highlight, no shade: the fill or the 1px edge is the only cue.

A. Soft lift

A 1px inner highlight along the top edge and a soft, short shade below. Pressed swaps both for a faint inner shade. In dark, the highlight carries the depth, because a shadow on near-black is not seen.

Measured: Labels sit on the flat tokens, so the lowest label pair stays 4.70:1. Focus ring against the shadowed gap (worst pixel): 3.23:1 on canvas, 3.04:1 on surface.

B. Gentle gradient

The fill darkens towards the bottom edge, by 10% ink in light and 14% in dark. The top stop is the token itself, so the measured pair is the floor. Pressed flips the gradient. No shadows.

Measured: Worst stop is the top stop, the token: 4.70:1. Bottom stops 5.44:1 (light) and 5.98:1 (dark). Dark outlined, lighter top stop: link 5.36:1, text 14.45:1.

C. Hairline bevel

Two inner 1px lines: lighter on top, darker at the bottom. Nothing outside the border box. Pressed inverts the bevel. The most precise and the closest to the current flat language.

Measured: Labels sit on the flat tokens: 4.70:1. Nothing outside the border, so the focus ring keeps 4.70:1 (light) and 7.27:1 (dark) on canvas.

D. Grounded

A soft ambient drop shadow and a darker 1px bottom edge, so the button sits on the page. Hover lifts the shadow a little, pressed removes it. In dark the top edge gets lighter instead.

Measured: Labels sit on the flat tokens: 4.70:1. In light, the darker bottom edge only raises the boundary (7.51:1 or more). In dark, the lighter top edge is 5.78:1 or more, and the bottom keeps the token edge (a darker one fell to 1.80:1). Focus ring against the shadowed gap: 3.23:1 on canvas, 3.04:1 on surface.

E. Tactile press

A 2px solid ledge in a darker shade of the fill, and the button moves down onto it when pressed (as GOV.UK does). The strongest "this is a button" cue, and the only one that changes position.

Measured: Labels sit on the flat tokens: 4.70:1. The ring offset is 4px here (2px past the ledge), so it keeps 4.70:1 (light) and 7.27:1 (dark) against canvas on both sides.
