Toast timer ring

Prototype for docs/design/toast-timer-ring.md. A ring inside Close drains over the toast's time. Hover or Tab into any toast and every ring freezes (the pause is page-wide). On resume, a ring with less than 5 s left jumps up to 5 s. The ring is aria-hidden; Close keeps its name and a 44 × 44 target (32 × 32 compact).

Timers running

No timer: a toast with an action

An action toast never times out, so it has no ring and Close looks as today. The same holds for autoDismiss: false. The ring's presence is the "closes by itself" cue.
