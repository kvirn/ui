# Icon library: grid, parts kit, file format and inventory

The construction facts for `@kvirn-ui/icons` (Plan 0124). The fixed numbers (grid, keylines, gaps, markup rules, angles) are in [`icon.md` §6.1](icon.md#61-drawing-rules-for-the-built-ins); how to decide and check is the `iconography` skill. This file holds what the two share in practice: the grid files, the parts with their coordinates, the file format, the inventory and the measured QA facts.

## 1. The grid

Three files in `packages/icons/grid/`. They are design-tool assets, not icons: the generator reads only `svg/`.

| File                 | What it is                                                                                                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `icon-grid.svg`      | The construction grid, 24 × 24 units (rendered at 960px). Import it as a locked bottom layer                    |
| `icon-grid-fine.svg` | The 0.5 and 0.25 sub-grid only, transparent. Lay it over the grid when you place or check a point               |
| `icon-template.svg`  | The starter: the root attributes from §6.1 and one placeholder dot at 12,12 (so editors keep the path). Copy it |

`icon-grid-fine.svg` has `grid-0-25-unit` (faintest) and `grid-0-5-unit` (a step darker, so half units stand out).

**How to use it.** Draw at stroke 1.5 on the grid, from a keyline and the parts below. Keylines are the outer limits of a shape's centreline, not a size every icon must fill.

> **Align to the grid as best you can, always.** Every horizontal and vertical centreline sits on a whole or half unit. Every endpoint, corner and arc end sits on the 0.25 grid. Any exception is justified in the construction note (§5).

Accepted exceptions: a dot ends at `+0.01`, and a dot is a point on the 0.25 grid (`warning`'s dot at y 16.75); the triangle keyline (§6.1); an arc radius is a length, not a coordinate, and may have two decimals (`eye`, r 9.48); arc break points may sit within 0.02 of the arc they split.

**Sizes.** A meaningful or status icon is at least **20px**; 16px is for decorative icons beside text (§6.1, maintainer 2026-10-16). Stroke 1 to 2 holds at 20px and up. At 16px, stroke 1.5 at most: at stroke 2 the globe and the document's text lines fill in.

## 4. File format

One file per icon, `packages/icons/svg/<name>.svg`, kebab-case, two-space indent, one element per line, final newline.

```svg
<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.5" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round">
  <path d="M6 4h12a2 2 0 0 1 2 2v12a2 2 0 0 1-2 2h-12a2 2 0 0 1-2-2v-12a2 2 0 0 1 2-2Z"/>
</svg>
```

- Root attributes exactly as above, and no others. Shapes carry only their geometry and inherit everything else.
- Prefer `path`: one per connected stroke. Absolute `M`, then relative commands; at most two decimals; closed shapes end in `Z`. Two parts are one path with two subpaths on purpose: the large dot (ring and centre dot) and `pin` (teardrop and hole, so the CSS fill keeps the hole).
- A closed shape starts at its top-left straight edge and runs clockwise. An open path starts at the end nearer the top-left. A full circle is two half arcs from its top.
- None of: `id`, `class`, `style`, `transform`, `opacity`, `fill` or `stroke` on a shape, `defs`, `use`, `mask`, `clip-path`, `title`, comments, editor metadata.
- Mirroring is not in the file: it is the name's entry in `packages/icons/mirror-in-rtl.json`.

**Gaps are measured on the centre-lines** (2026-10-17). The earlier claim here (smallest gap 1.88, `council-meeting` 1.94 "by construction", from a pixel measure) was wrong: a render counts strokes that are joined elsewhere as **one shape**, so it can't see two strokes of one connected drawing come close. The rule: two separate strokes keep their centre-lines **at least 3.5 apart** (2 painted clear at stroke 1.5); a dot keeps **3.0** (1.5 clear). A pixel render is only a second check (the 16px fill-in test).

`packages/icons/scripts/check-gaps.mjs` measures it: `node packages/icons/scripts/check-gaps.mjs [svg directory]` prints one line per pair under the limit and exits 1 if there is one; `packages/icons/src/gaps.test.ts` proves its rules. It flattens each path in 0.25 steps and compares commands, with eight rules for what is a join, not a gap:

1. **Painted join:** an endpoint under 0.75 from another stroke's centre-line is painted into it (`help` ticks, `gender-male` shaft, `search` and `lost-property` handles, `contrast` stripes).
2. **Shared joint:** two paths that meet or cross and leave each other at 45° or more are one join; pairs within 5 of it (one arm length) pass (`refresh` and `history` heads, `bike` joints, `train` rails).
3. **Arc points touch at 0.2:** a point on an arc may sit that far off the stroke it joins, for a 2-decimal radius (the `bike` rims, r 3.54).
4. **Hairpin only:** a stroke is compared with itself only as a hairpin, a semicircle's two ends one diameter apart.
5. **Wedge:** a straight run, a link at least 3.5 long and a third stroke of one path pass when one of them is 45°.
6. **Rounded corner:** a corner arc is compared only at its ends; its straight edges keep the gap.
7. **Neighbouring crossings:** commands that cross or share an endpoint in neighbouring commands are a join (the `marriage` rings).
8. **Tangent to a ring:** a stroke tangent to a ring skips the ring's far half.

**Accepted exceptions (the ALLOW list, exact by icon, by the two strokes and by distance):** `settings` (gear teeth 2.0 apart, by design); `delete` (the pilot handle, 3 pairs with the lid, 2.5); `train` and `tram` (the window slot, opposite edges 3.0 apart: a named slot); and the **named near-miss tolerance** for `ambulance` 3.0, `signature` 3.0, `warning` 3.1, `quick-exit` about 3.0 and `bike` 3.3 (centre-line). A clear gap of 1.5 to 2.0 is allowed only for those pairs: a meaningful icon is at least 20px, where 1.5 units is 1.25px and the gap stays open (design review, decision 4). Any gap under 1.5 clear is a redraw.

Today the checker prints nothing for all 257 icons it was run on (2026-10-17, `spinner` included). **It is not a gate for the set:** no `vp run icons:check` and no CI step runs it over the set until the maintainer decides (a gate change). `packages/icons/src/gaps.test.ts` asserts in `vp test` that `ambulance`, `signature`, `warning`, `quick-exit` and `bike` stay clean, so the five tolerances are tested; the rest of the set is not a gate.

**Contact sheets** (temporary, in `/tmp/kv-icons-spec242`): the whole set at 24px light and dark, 20px, 16px greyscale, and the batch 3 and variant pairs at 16px greyscale and 20px dark, grouped by batch beside the pilot and batch 2. **Sizes.** A meaningful or status icon (one with a label key) is used at **20px or larger**; 16px is for decorative icons beside text only (§1, `icon.md` §6.1).
