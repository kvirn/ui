# Icon library: grid, parts kit, file format and inventory

The construction facts for `@kvirn-ui/icons` (Plan 0124). The fixed numbers (grid, keylines, gaps, markup rules, angles) are in [`icon.md` §6.1](icon.md#61-drawing-rules-for-the-built-ins); how to decide and check is the `iconography` skill. This file holds what the two share in practice: the grid files, the parts with their coordinates, the file format, the inventory and the measured QA facts.

## 1. The grid

Three files in `packages/icons/grid/`. They are design-tool assets, not icons: the generator reads only `svg/`.

| File                 | What it is                                                                                                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `icon-grid.svg`      | The construction grid, 24 × 24 units (rendered at 960px). Import it as a locked bottom layer                    |
| `icon-grid-fine.svg` | The 0.5 and 0.25 sub-grid only, transparent. Lay it over the grid when you place or check a point               |
| `icon-template.svg`  | The starter: the root attributes from §6.1 and one placeholder dot at 12,12 (so editors keep the path). Copy it |

`icon-grid-fine.svg` has `grid-0-25-unit` (faintest) and `grid-0-5-unit` (a step darker, so half units stand out).

**How to use it.** Draw at stroke 1.5 on the grid, from a keyline and the parts below. Keylines are the outer limits of a shape's centreline, not a size every icon must fill.

> **Align to the grid as best you can, always.** Every horizontal and vertical centreline sits on a whole or half unit. Every endpoint, corner and arc end sits on the 0.25 grid. Any exception is justified in the construction note (§5).

Accepted exceptions: a dot ends at `+0.01`, and a dot is a point on the 0.25 grid (`warning`'s dot at y 16.75); the triangle keyline (§6.1); an arc radius is a length, not a coordinate, and may have two decimals (`eye`, r 9.48); arc break points may sit within 0.02 of the arc they split.

**Sizes.** A meaningful or status icon is at least **20px**; 16px is for decorative icons beside text (§6.1, maintainer 2026-10-16). Stroke 1 to 2 holds at 20px and up. At 16px, stroke 1.5 at most: at stroke 2 the globe and the document's text lines fill in.

## 4. File format

One file per icon, `packages/icons/svg/<name>.svg`, kebab-case, two-space indent, one element per line, final newline.

```svg
<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.5" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round">
  <path d="M6 4h12a2 2 0 0 1 2 2v12a2 2 0 0 1-2 2h-12a2 2 0 0 1-2-2v-12a2 2 0 0 1 2-2Z"/>
</svg>
```

- Root attributes exactly as above, and no others. Shapes carry only their geometry and inherit everything else.
- Prefer `path`: one per connected stroke. Absolute `M`, then relative commands; at most two decimals; closed shapes end in `Z`. Two parts are one path with two subpaths on purpose: the large dot (ring and centre dot) and `pin` (teardrop and hole, so the CSS fill keeps the hole).
- A closed shape starts at its top-left straight edge and runs clockwise. An open path starts at the end nearer the top-left. A full circle is two half arcs from its top.
- None of: `id`, `class`, `style`, `transform`, `opacity`, `fill` or `stroke` on a shape, `defs`, `use`, `mask`, `clip-path`, `title`, comments, editor metadata.
- Mirroring is not in the file: it is the name's entry in `packages/icons/mirror-in-rtl.json`.

## 7. QA facts

**Ink density** is the share of pixels at alpha ≥ 50% in a 24px icon rendered 8× (192px, stroke 1.5, Chromium). It is judged against the median of the icon's class, **±40%, a warning, not a gate** (maintainer 2026-10-16): outside the band, look at the contact sheet, then redraw or justify the icon here.

| Class    | What                                                                                                                              | Reference median                                               |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `open`   | Strokes with no enclosing outline: chevrons, arrows, ×, +, −, check, `code`, loop arrows, route and line handles                  | 0.0666: the 10 pilot open glyphs as measured (`menu` included) |
| `lines`  | Built from parallel rules and bars: `menu`, lists, alignment, `quote`, `sort`, corner marks, `bold`, `italic`, `clear-formatting` | 0.1476: the 17 drawn (the pilot has only `menu`)               |
| `dots`   | Large dots only: `more`, `more-vertical`, `drag-handle`                                                                           | 0.0247: the 3 drawn                                            |
| `object` | An enclosing outline or a closed object                                                                                           | 0.1781: the 15 pilot objects                                   |

**Outside the band** (measured 2026-10-16 on all 84; a warning each, accepted by the lead unless marked **new**, found at consolidation and not yet decided):

| Icon                         | Class  | Density        | Against the class | Why it stays                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------- | ------ | -------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `language`                   | object | 0.2986         | +68%              | The circle and meridian alone give about 0.25; an equator-only globe read less like a globe (§5)                             |
| `table`                      | object | 0.2684         | +51%              | Frame, two rules and a column are the grid (family C2)                                                                       |
| `table-merge`, `table-split` | object | 0.2601, 0.2721 | +46%, +53%        | Two arrows inside the frame; split is merge mirrored with a whole column (family C2; split **new** at +53% since its redraw) |
| `settings`                   | object | 0.2570         | +44%              | The teeth are the gear; accepted like `language` (family D)                                                                  |
| `undo`, `redo`               | open   | 0.1154, 0.1153 | +73%              | A loop arrow carries a half circle besides its head: loop arrows are their own case (family C1)                              |
| `directions`                 | open   | 0.1088         | +63%              | A start ring, a route across the diagonal and a head: the ends are the metaphor (family F)                                   |
| `distance`                   | open   | 0.1027         | +54%              | Three handle rings on the full diagonal (family F)                                                                           |
| `remove`                     | open   | 0.0395         | −41%              | **New.** One 14-unit stroke, the kit's −; it can't carry more ink                                                            |
| `more`, `more-vertical`      | dots   | 0.0247         | −63% against open | Three dots are the glyph: the reason the `dots` class exists; at the dots median                                             |
| `drag-handle`                | dots   | 0.0496         | +100%             | **New.** Six dots against three, by construction                                                                             |
| `bold`, `italic`             | lines  | 0.1847, 0.1789 | +25%, +21%        | Inside as `lines`. Family A measured them as `open` (+177%, +169%); they are line-built, so this file classes them `lines`   |

Every other icon is inside its band. The open reference is bimodal (chevrons 0.0545, arrows 0.0914): against the median of the 19 open icons drawn (0.0914) the chevrons sit at −40%.

**Live area and gaps (batch 2, 8× render).** `link` and `link-off` paint to 2–22, the live-area limit; `pin` and `pin-add` paint to y 22 at the tip. Nothing crosses it. Smallest gap between separate strokes: 1.97 (the pin's hole, the gear's hub: 2 by construction; the pixel measure is ±0.13).

**Sizes.** A meaningful or status icon (one with a label key) is used at **20px or larger**; 16px is for decorative icons beside text only (§1, `icon.md` §6.1).

**Originality.** No path string equals one in Heroicons or Lucide (grep before hand-off). Single straight segments and dots are exempt (maintainer 2026-10-16): on this grid they have one spelling (`remove`'s `M5 12h14`, the rule lines, the plain dot). Every path with two or more segments or a curve must be unique.
