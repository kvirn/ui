# Design spec: ReadAloud player and selection trigger

<!-- kv-lang-ok-file: the content table gives each string in Swedish and names the Finnish length check -->

- **Status:** Draft (revision 3) · **Designer:** ux-designer agent · **Date:** 2026-10-25 · supersedes revision 2 (2026-10-25) and the 2026-10-07 draft
- **Plan:** [0153](plans/0153-read-aloud-kvirn-only.md) (T1), revising [0088](plans/0088-read-aloud.md) (T1) and [0151](plans/0151-read-aloud-service.md) (T4) · **Type:** component default styling, states and copy
- **What changed in this revision:** the anatomy, not the behaviour. Revision 2 kept the prototype's shape (one wrapping row of four identical secondary buttons), which is unopinionated rather than designed. This revision gives the player **a panel, a hierarchy and a position meter**: Listen is the one filled button, Previous and Next are a tight cluster, Stop is set apart by space, an `aria-hidden` sentence meter sits on top, and every control carries an icon **beside** its word. Three new parts and four part classes (§5), **no new token, no new colour pair, no new i18n key**.
- **Unchanged and kept:** in the content flow under the `h1` — never floating, never sticky, never in the mobile menu. Words on every control. `preparing` announced once past 1 s and never per sentence; `partSkipped` visible only; one polite region. The highlight is a reading aid, never on `:hover`/`:focus`. No auto-start.

## 1. Brief

- **Users:** residents reading an article, decision or letter. **Hardest case:** a resident with dyslexia or low literacy, reading in their second language on a 320px phone over a slow mobile connection, who has never used a read-aloud tool; second, a magnifier user at 400% who sees only part of the player.
- **Job:** "When a page is long or hard, I want to hear it while I follow the words, so I understand it without asking someone."
- **Context:** once per visit, often on a phone, usually while also reading. It must not fight a screen reader: screen-reader users already have a better reader and are **not** the audience (0088, Non-goals).
- **Constraints:** WCAG 2.2 AA; GDPR art. 25 (the sentence leaves the browser, so the resident is told in words who makes the audio and that nothing is stored); AGENTS.md rule 7 exception D5 (0151): the text goes to `voice.kvirn.com` only after a press. The service is a single EU host, is CPU-bound and can be busy.
- **Success:** the resident starts, pauses and finishes listening without help; can say which sentence is being read; and when the service is slow or down, knows it is the service and what to do next.
- **Evidence:** text-to-speech gives readers with reading disabilities a small-to-moderate comprehension gain (meta-analyses: Wood et al. 2018, d = 0.35, n = 2,942; Perelmutter et al. 2017, g = 0.445) and measurably less mind-wandering (Bonifacci 2022). Benefit is uneven: some readers do worse with it, so it is always optional and never on by default. Everything about **our** player is an assumption (A) until the sessions in §8.
- **Assumption → research question:** (A) residents look for Listen under the `h1` → do they find it there, or at the top of the page chrome, where Swedish municipalities put it? (A) a filled Listen reads as "the thing to press" without competing with the page's own primary action → does it? (A) a meter with no handle is read as information, not as a seek bar → or do residents try to drag it?

## 3. Flow

1. The resident lands on the article. The player sits under the `h1` as one panel (§5). Nothing plays and nothing is fetched: the voice list and the first sentence are requested only by a press (0151 D5, 1.4.2). The meter is empty.
2. Presses **Listen**. The audio element is unlocked inside that gesture (iOS), sentence 1 is requested. Listen becomes **Pause** at once; the resident can stop the wait at any moment.
3. Under ~300 ms nothing else changes. Past 300 ms the `play` icon becomes a spinner and `preparing` appears in Status; past ~1 s `preparing` is announced once (politely). The meter does not move: nothing has been read yet.
4. Audio starts: the highlight lands on sentence 1, Status shows "Sentence 1 of 12", the meter fills to 1/12, and sentence 2 is fetched while 1 plays. Nothing is announced.
5. **Pause** pauses the audio where it is and keeps the highlight, the meter and the position. Listen resumes mid-sentence (0153 D7). **Previous / Next** move one sentence, playing or paused. **Stop** clears the highlight, the meter and Status, back to idle at sentence 1.
6. **Speed** applies to the sentence already playing (`playbackRate`, pitch preserved): no refetch, no gap. **Voice** (where rendered) restarts the current sentence in the new voice.
7. End of the content: back to idle, silently; the meter empties, because nothing is being read. Focus never moves.
8. **Selection:** the resident selects text. Listen becomes **Listen to selected text**; with a pointer, a button with the same words appears by the selection (§6). Either reads only the selection.

**Unhappy paths.** Every one of them keeps the player where it is, keeps the position and the meter, and offers the same next step: press Listen again.

## 5. Structure

**Where it sits.** Inside `main`, in the article, after the `h1` and its date line and before the lead. Outside `contentRef`, or skipped by it (`data-kv-read-aloud-skip`), so the player never reads its own words. **Never sticky, never floating, never in the mobile menu, for this inline row** (DESIGN.md Layout; 2.4.11; 400% zoom) — unchanged by 0154. The one approved exception is a `ReadAloud.Root` that contains a `ReadAloud.Bar` (0154 D10, D39): sticky positioning is on that Root, not the Bar itself, and only while it is playing, never while idle; it un-sticks at `(max-height: 30rem)` instead of disappearing. **This means the Bar's Root must contain only the Bar and sit as a direct child of a page-tall element** — nested inside something shorter the sticky range collapses to nothing, wrapped around the whole page the page itself becomes the sticky box, and both failure modes look like "sticky is broken" rather than "Root is in the wrong place." One inline row per `contentRef`. Put it outside `kv-prose`, or in `kv-not-prose`, so prose margins don't reach inside it.

**Superseded 2026-10-26 (0154 D13, D38–D40):** the panel described below as always-chromed (surface fill, border, radius, padding on every state) is cast away. Before the first press, the panel is chromeless — no border, no fill, no padding, just the Listen button in the flow — and gains the panel look only once a resident has pressed it (D13). `ReadAloud.Bar` is never a card at all, in any state: when a Bar is mounted inside `ReadAloud.Root`, Root paints nothing and the Bar itself is the full-width band (D38, D39). Where the rest of §5 and §6 below describes "the panel" as always framed, read it as the framing that appears after the first press, not before.

main

      button.kv-button.kv-read-aloud-button.kv-read-aloud-next      [skip-next] Next sentence
    button.kv-button.kv-read-aloud-button.kv-read-aloud-stop        [stop] Stop

    p.kv-read-aloud-note    Listening sends this text to Kvirn … a.kv-link How listening works
  div (contentRef)  p.lead … article text …

DOM order = visual order = Tab order. No `order`, no reversing. **Recommended composition (resident pages):** Progress, Play, Transport (Previous, Next), Stop, Settings, Status, Note. **Speed and Voice are not rendered inline** — both moved into `ReadAloud.Settings`'s popup (0154 D8), so there is one settings layer, not two. Every part stays optional: without Progress or Transport the row still lays out, with the plain gap between the buttons.

**Three zones, read left to right:** the one filled action (Listen) · the sentence cluster (Previous + Next, 8px apart, one flex child so they never split across lines) · Stop, set apart by 36px, so a hand reaching for Next never lands on it. Then the Speed setting, then two rows of words.

**No voice for the page's language.** The player renders nothing: no panel, no buttons, no message. A dead Listen button that always fails is worse than none, and a permanent "no Finnish voice" line on every page of a Finnish site is an apology on every page. The hook exposes `hasVoice: false`, so the site can say it once where it chooses — in its accessibility statement, or as `errorNoVoice` beside the article. The voice list only arrives after the first press (0151 D5), so until then the player renders in full; a consumer that knows its content language can pass it and avoid even that.

### 5a. What `ReadAloud.Bar` reads (Plan 0154 D11, D15)

A site-wide bar has no `contentRef` of its own by default, so the resolution order is **`contentRef` → `scope` → `<main>`**: the consumer's own ref wins, else a CSS selector (`scope="#article"`), else the page's `<main>` landmark — the one default that needs no per-route wiring, since every page here already has exactly one `main`. A missing `main` with no `contentRef` and no matching `scope` reads nothing and warns once in development.

**Within whichever root wins**, `<!-- KV:READ-ALOUD:START -->` / `<!-- KV:READ-ALOUD:STOP -->` HTML comment pairs narrow it further: with any `START` present, only the marked ranges are read; with none, the whole root is, as before. Several pairs are allowed, so a page can have more than one readable region, and `scope` and the markers compose — a `scope` selector still respects markers inside it. The markers exist for what `data-kv-read-aloud-skip` cannot express: a **mid-flow range that doesn't align with any element**. Comparing the two:

|             | `data-kv-read-aloud-skip` | `KV:READ-ALOUD:START` / `STOP` |
| ----------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Direction   | Excludes an element       | Includes a range               |
| Granularity | A whole element           | Any text, mid-element          |
| Author      | React/JSX or markup       | Markup only (below)            |

**Two limits, not worked around:**

1. **React/JSX cannot emit an HTML comment**, so the marker pair is for server-rendered and CMS markup — SiteVision, Optimizely, Drupal, WordPress and the like. A React consumer reaches for `contentRef` or `data-kv-read-aloud-skip` instead.
2. **An HTML minifier strips comments by default.** A production build that minifies markup removes the markers with them, and the bar then silently reads the whole resolved root. Keep comments on, or don't rely on the markers in a minified build.

An unclosed `START` reads to the end of the root; an orphan `STOP` is ignored; nested or overlapping pairs flatten to the outermost one. Each of the three warns once in development — reading the wrong half of a page silently is worse than a console warning.

**The meter: informative, not seekable (recommendation).** Sentence index over total is all the machine has; there is no intra-sentence time. A real seek control would have to be a `slider` with Arrow/Home/End/PageUp/PageDown keys, `aria-valuetext` in words, a pointer alternative (2.5.7) and its own contract rows and tests — and at 288px wide with 12 sentences each step is 24px, so the hardest-case user is the one most likely to lose their place with it. Previous and Next already seek by sentence, with 44px targets and words on them. So the meter is decorative and silent, Status carries the information, and the cost of changing our mind later is a new part with a slider contract, not a restyle. **Risk:** a bar at the top of a player invites a drag. Mitigated by no handle, no hover and no cursor change, and tested in §8.

### Current-sentence highlight `::highlight(kv-read-aloud)` — unchanged from revision 1

`::highlight()` takes only colour, background and decoration, so the look is one fill: `primary` with `on-primary` text in all four themes, `Highlight` / `HighlightText` in forced colours. Measured (WCAG formula on palette values; `theme:check` stays the source): fill vs surroundings 4.70 canvas / 4.42 surface (light), 4.44 / 4.05 / 3.75 (dark), 9.89 (light-contrast), 11.14 (dark-contrast); text on fill 4.70 and up. Both pairs are already in `theme:check`.

- **Rejected:** a soft `primary-subtle` tint (calmer to read, A) is 1.13:1 on canvas in light, so the sentence isn't told from its neighbours at low vision.
- `color: on-primary` recolours a link inside the sentence too, so no link sits at ~1.2:1 on the fill. Story check: a sentence with a link, four themes.
- It is **a reading aid, not a status**: a highlight only ever appears while audio for that sentence is playing or paused, never on a buffer gap, an error, `:hover` or **`:focus`**. Position in words is in Status (1.4.1).
- It paints a range, so no element is wrapped, nothing reflows and the consumer's own styles are untouched. `::selection` paints above it, so reading a selection collapses that selection first (focus does not move).

### Modes

- **Forced colours:** the panel is `Canvas` with a 1px `CanvasText` edge (every surface keeps an edge). The meter: `Canvas` track, `CanvasText` edge, `Highlight` fill — the same rule `kv-progress-bar` uses. Buttons, selects and the spinner keep their forced rules; icons take their parent's system colour. Status and Note `CanvasText`, and the words carry every status.
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` or `data-kv-motion="reduce"`): the spinner shows its rest arc and does not turn; the words still appear. The meter never animates in any mode. Scroll-follow is **instant, not off**: a jump is not animation, and turning it off loses a reduced-motion reader's place on every sentence. Follow stops after a manual scroll until the next Listen, Previous or Next.
- **2.2.2:** the only looping thing is the spinner, and a wait is bounded by the 10 s request budget; Pause stops both the wait and the spinner at any moment, which is the mechanism 2.2.2 asks for.
- **320px, 400%, 1.4.12:** no fixed widths or heights on text; buttons, Status and Note wrap and hyphenate; Listen and Speed are full width and the transport is two equal columns below `40rem`. At 400% (288px of panel, 256px inside) "Previous sentence" wraps to two lines and both cluster buttons grow together.

## 7. Accessibility annotations

Draft input for `read-aloud.a11y.md`.

- **Tab stops, in order:** Play → Previous → Next → Stop → Speed (→ Voice, where rendered) → the Note's link. **Unchanged by this revision:** the panel, the meter, the transport wrapper and the icons add no stop. Status, the spinner and the highlight are never focusable. No player rendered = no Tab stops.
- **Keys** (`keyboard` skill): Enter and Space on the buttons (native), native `<select>` keys, Escape inside Root stops the audio and aborts the request with focus unmoved, Escape dismisses the selection trigger. No page-level shortcut, and no key on the meter, because it is not a control.
- **Roles and names:** Root `role="group"` named by `readAloud.label`; the panel look adds no role, heading or landmark. Buttons named by their visible text, with the icon `aria-hidden` so the name is exactly the word (2.5.3); selects by their `<label for>`; Status a `<p role="status" aria-live="off">` — findable, silent; the spinner and the meter `aria-hidden`; Note plain text plus a `kv-link`.
- **Focus:** never moves — not on start, pause, stop, the end of the text, a state change or an error. Never trapped. The focus ring sits `--kv-focus-ring-offset` outside each button, inside the panel's padding, so it is never clipped (`overflow` is only on the meter). The selection trigger never takes focus (`tabindex="-1"`, `pointerdown` prevented, not `aria-hidden`) and any key dismisses it before focus moves (2.4.11).
- **Announcements** (Announcer, polite, each at most once per press): `preparing` only when the wait passes 1 s; `errorServiceBusy`; `errorServiceUnavailable`; `errorPlayback`. **Nothing else.** Not play, pause, next, stop, the end, each sentence, a position change, a meter step, a mid-text gap, a skipped part or a speed change. A live region that narrates sentences talks over both the audio and a screen reader and is a defect.
- **Colour independence (1.4.1):** every state is in words in Status; the icons differ in shape (triangle, bars, square, bar-and-triangle) as well as position; the meter is length, not hue, and duplicates words that are already there; the highlight is a luminance change plus the position in text.
- **Target size:** native controls at `--kv-control-min-block-size` (44px comfortable, 32px compact, never under 24×24, 2.5.8); the 8px gap inside the transport cluster separates two full-height targets; the selection trigger 44px.
- **Language (3.1.2):** each chunk is read by a voice for its own `lang`, or skipped. Never Finnish in a Swedish voice.
- **SCs of note:** 1.4.1, **1.4.2** (nothing starts without a press, and Pause and Stop exist anyway), 1.4.10, 1.4.11, 1.4.12, 2.1.1, **2.2.2** (Pause stops the only loop), 2.4.4, 2.4.11, 2.5.3, 2.5.8, 3.1.2, 4.1.2, 4.1.3.
