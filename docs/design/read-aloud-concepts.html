Building permit for a deck

Updated 3 October 2026

You need a permit if the deck is more than 1.2 metres above the ground. The fee is 2 400 kr and is paid when the decision is sent. Apply at least four weeks before you start building.

Fees

The fee covers the review and one site visit. If the deck is rebuilt, a new permit is needed.
